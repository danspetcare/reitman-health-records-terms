Reitman Health Records — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Reitman Health Records is a personal, single-user tool operated by Daniel Reitman to retrieve his own medical records from healthcare organizations that use Epic's patient-access interface. This policy explains what the app does with that information.

Information the app accesses

Only the health information you choose to share on the healthcare organization's authorization screen, limited to the categories Epic makes available to patient-facing apps (for example demographics, conditions, medications, allergies, immunizations, visits, procedures, results, vital signs, care plans, insurance information, clinical notes and documents).

How it is used

Solely so the account holder can keep a personal copy of his own records and review them. The app does not analyze, aggregate across people, or use the information for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

On the author's personal computer only, in a folder readable only by the author's user account. The app sends requests only to the healthcare organization's own FHIR server. No information is transmitted to the author's servers, cloud services, analytics providers, advertisers, or any other third party, because none are used.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell your data.
  • It does not share your data with anyone.
  • It does not use your data for marketing or advertising.
  • It does not use your data to gain insights about your family or anyone else.
  • It does not keep access tokens or refresh tokens; every run requires a new sign-in.

Retention and deletion

Saved records remain on the author's computer until the author deletes them. The author can delete them at any time.

Your choices

You decide which categories to share on the authorization screen, and you can revoke the app's access at any time in the healthcare organization's patient portal (for example, MyChart's linked-apps settings).

HIPAA note

The app is not a healthcare provider or business associate and is not covered by HIPAA. The protections described here are commitments made by the app's author.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the author at the email address shown on the healthcare organization's app-authorization screen.
