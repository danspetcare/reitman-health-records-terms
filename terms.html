Reitman Health Records — Terms and Conditions

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Reitman Health Records ("the app") is a personal, single-user software tool written and operated by Daniel Reitman for the sole purpose of retrieving his own medical records from healthcare organizations that use Epic's patient-access (SMART on FHIR) interface. It is not offered to the public, is not a commercial product, and has no users other than its author.

1. Who may use the app

The app is intended only for Daniel Reitman to access records belonging to him (or records he is legally authorized to access through a healthcare organization's proxy-access rules). No other person is granted a license to use it.

2. What the app does

When you authorize the app through a healthcare organization's patient portal (for example, MyChart), the app retrieves the clinical data you chose to share, such as conditions, medications, allergies, immunizations, visits, procedures, lab results, vital signs, clinical notes and documents, and saves it to a folder on the author's personal computer. The app runs locally on that computer.

3. Data handling

  • Records are stored only on the author's personal computer. They are never uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party.
  • The app does not sell, share, license, advertise with, or otherwise disclose any health information.
  • The app does not use your data to gain insights about anyone other than the account holder.
  • The app does not store access tokens; it requires a fresh sign-in to the healthcare organization each time it runs.
  • Data may be retained indefinitely on the author's computer and can be deleted by the author at any time.

4. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time through the healthcare organization's patient portal (for example, the "Linked Apps and Devices" or similar settings page in MyChart). Revoking access does not delete data already saved on the author's computer.

5. No medical advice

The app only retrieves and displays records produced by your healthcare providers. It does not interpret them and does not provide medical advice. Always consult your healthcare provider about your health.

The app is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. To the fullest extent permitted by law, the author is not liable for any damages arising from use of the app, including incomplete or inaccurate data returned by a healthcare organization's systems.

8. Contact

Questions about these terms or the app's data handling can be sent to the author at the email address shown on the healthcare organization's app-authorization screen.
